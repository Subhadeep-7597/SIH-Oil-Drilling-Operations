## IDEA & SOLUTION

**NWIS (Nearby Wells Intelligence System)** is an ***AI-powered decision-support platform alongside eRTMAC***, bringing offset-well experience to the depth being drilled now.

- **AI document intelligence:** **OCR + NLP** turn decades of WCRs, DDRs and scans into a ***searchable well knowledge base***.
- **Geospatial offset-well map:** wells within a chosen radius, with **losses, kicks, stuck pipe and NPT** marked.
- **Cross-well correlation:** compares wells **by depth and formation** and ranks the most similar offset wells.
- **Real-time eRTMAC integration:** matches **live depth and drilling parameters** against offset-well history.
- **Predictive risk forecasting:** **ML + anomaly detection** give ***early warnings before risky depths***, with risk score and confidence.
- **Explainable AI:** every alert shows ***why it appeared***, with evidence wells and **links to source reports**.
- **MCP-based AI assistant:** answers through **audited, read-only data tools**, so answers are ***traceable, never invented***.
- **Low-connectivity support:** **offline well pack** on rig tablets; actions **sync when the network returns**.

**Impact:** faster, safer decisions · fewer repeated incidents · less lost rig time · ***drilling knowledge preserved for the next generation***

---

## PROBLEM RESOLUTION

**Problem:** Drilling knowledge is **scattered across reports, scans and experts' memory**. Searching takes **hours**, so ***past warnings surface only after problems repeat***. Records are **inconsistent**, rigs have **weak networks**, and data is **too sensitive for public AI**.

**Solution:** NWIS ***automatically builds a searchable knowledge base***, **ranks relevant offset wells** and ***warns before risky depths*** with **source-linked evidence**. Data is **standardised and expert-reviewed**, key history is **available offline at the rig**, and everything ***runs inside OIL's network***.

---

## UNIQUENESS

Existing tools each cover **one piece**: document stores, live dashboards, maps or chatbots. NWIS ***connects them around the well being drilled now***, comparing **live conditions with OIL's own history** and warning before known trouble, ***always with evidence***. Its AI works only through **audited, read-only MCP tools**, so it ***cannot invent facts***, and it runs **entirely inside OIL's network**. Field staff can ***ask by voice in Hindi or Assamese*** (self-hosted **Whisper + IndicTrans2**), which brings OIL's drilling history to staff that **English-only tools leave out**.
